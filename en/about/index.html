YEVHENII MURADOV

Between craft and technology

An original approach to jewellery, sculpture and objects where an idea travels the full path into physical form.

Approach

Precision does not exclude character.

Yevhenii Muradov works where artistic form, traditional craft and contemporary digital production meet.

A project may begin with a hand sketch, photograph, short description or an existing 3D model. The aim is not to impose a form on the material, but to find a method in which concept, construction and technology support one another.

International projects

Working with recognisable subjects

Completed work includes a stone sculpture of Conor McGregor, a portrait bust for Richard Hammond, scale models and original art objects.
